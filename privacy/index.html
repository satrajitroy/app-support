App Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to apps that list this page as their privacy policy.

These apps are designed so that they run locally on the user’s iPhone or iPad. Unless an app-specific support page states otherwise, no account or sign-in is required.

Data entered into the apps

These apps allow users to enter or create app-specific information, saved states, profiles, rules, generated results, or exported files.

Users are responsible for deciding what information to enter into an app and where any exported or shared files are sent.

Local storage

App settings, saved states, profiles, and other app-generated data are stored locally on the user’s device where applicable.

The developer does not operate a server that these apps rely on for their functionality.

Network use

The apps covered by this policy do not require a network connection for their functionality unless an app-specific support page explicitly states otherwise.

They do not use advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, or remote configuration services.

Data leaves the device only when the user explicitly chooses to export or share content using iOS or iPadOS sharing features, or when the user independently chooses another destination or service. Those sharing actions are controlled by the user and by the selected destination.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through the apps covered by this policy.

The apps do not track users across apps or websites.

The developer does not sell user data.

Third-party services

The apps covered by this policy do not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

User control

Users can delete locally stored app data, saved states, profiles, or generated files from the app or from device storage where applicable.

Children and families

The apps covered by this policy are designed to be usable without advertising, tracking, accounts, online communication, or data collection.

The developer does not collect personal data from children through these apps.

App-specific information

If an individual app has data practices or requirements that differ from this general policy, those differences will be described on that app’s support page and this policy will be updated as needed.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the data practices of the covered apps change. The current version will remain available at this privacy policy URL.
